# V1 Product Specification

## Mission
Continuously find legitimate income opportunities that can be pursued with zero upfront capital, verify the evidence, determine eligibility, rank by earning fit, notify the user and track outcomes through payment.

## Priority order
1. Direct paid jobs/contracts/tasks
2. Fixed bounties / paid contributions
3. AI evaluation/training work
4. Paid testing/research
5. Web3 direct work and zero-capital opportunities
6. Grants / non-competitive programs
7. Airdrops/quests
8. Competitions/hackathons (optional; hidden by default)

## Target markets
Nigeria, United Kingdom, Canada and United States are first-class markets; global opportunities remain supported.

## Required filters
- $0 capital required
- Direct pay
- No CV required
- No portfolio required
- Country eligibility
- Remote/onsite
- Payment method
- Category
- Skills
- Deadline
- Risk/trust score
- Competition allowed/hidden

## Offline contract
Cache only user-visible opportunity data, saved evidence summaries, guides and application state. Never cache provider secrets, seed phrases, private keys or privileged service credentials. Queue safe user actions for sync after reconnect.
